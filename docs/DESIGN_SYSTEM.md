# Design System

## Two independent appearance layers

### Reading mode

- Dark
- Light

Reading mode controls the underlying paper and ink treatment.

### Regional atmosphere

- Windswept Hills
- Bamboo Groves
- Verdant Brook
- Obsidian Mountain
- Astral Mountains
- Dessicated Desert
- Sakurajima
- Feybreak

Regional atmosphere controls accent, depth, marks, and mood.

Light and dark must never replace regional themes.
